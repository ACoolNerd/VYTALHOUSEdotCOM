## 01. EVIDENTIARY CLASSIFICATION FRAMEWORK

Every factual statement, claim, figure, address, and specification within the VYTAL Corporate Document Ecosystem must be classified according to one of the seven official evidentiary statuses:

1. **`VERIFIED`**: Validated by executed legal contracts, physical leases, bank statements, or board resolutions.
2. **`USER PROVIDED`**: Supplied directly by Keith McPherson / Principal Leadership.
3. **`EXTERNAL SOURCE`**: Sourced from third-party peer-reviewed scientific journals, PubMed, FDA databases, or Maryland statutory codes.
4. **`ASSUMPTION`**: Working operational assumption used for financial modeling; subject to sensitivity testing.
5. **`PROJECTION`**: Forward-looking 3-year/5-year financial model output based on baseline unit economics.
6. **`PROPOSED`**: Conceptual strategy or architectural proposal under executive review.
7. **`REQUIRES VERIFICATION` (`[VERIFY]`)**: Unresolved item, undecided site choice, or pending legal/clinical sign-off (`⚑`).
