## 01. THE 11 OFFICIAL LOGO CONFIGURATIONS

The VYTAL logo system consists of exactly 11 approved configurations. No other variations are permitted.

1. **Config 01 — Master V Monogram**: 3D faceted Iridescent Platinum Silver V mark. Used on architectural hero backdrops, mobile app icons, and document covers.
2. **Config 02 — VYTAL HOUSE Primary Wordmark**: `VYTAL HOUSE` in extended geometric uppercase with `Renew • Restore • Recharge` sub-tagline and central heartbeat pulse divider.
3. **Config 03 — Horizontal Lockup**: Monogram + vertical divider line + `VYTAL HOUSE` wordmark. Used for website navigation headers.
4. **Config 04 — Stacked Lockup**: Centered 3D V Monogram positioned above `VYTAL HOUSE` wordmark and tagline.
5. **Config 05 — Circular Seal**: Concentric circular badge (`VYTAL HOUSE` • `OPTIMISE • ELEVATE • ENDURE` • `RENEW • RESTORE • RECHARGE`). Used for retail product seals and legal accreditation badges.
6. **Config 06 — App Icon**: 3D V Monogram centered in a squircle with a subtle Acid Lime heartbeat base glow.
7. **Config 07 — Social Avatar**: 3D V Monogram centered in a dark circular frame with a glowing heartbeat base.
8. **Config 08 — Favicon**: 32x32px micro V mark optimized for browser tabs.
9. **Config 09 — Monogram Badge**: Hexagonal metallic badge containing the 3D V mark. Used on physical membership cards and facility hardware.
10. **Config 10 — Simplified Micro-Mark**: Clean 2D outline V for small UI badges and low-resolution viewports.
11. **Config 11 — Divider / Heartbeat Motif**: Hairline horizontal energy line with a glowing pulse node used as a section divider.

## 02. PROPORTIONS, CLEAR SPACE & MINIMUM SIZES

- **Clear Space Rule**: Safe area surrounding the logo must equal X (the height of the wordmark or width of the inner V serif).
- **Minimum Sizes**:
  - Monogram alone: `16px` / `6mm` minimum height.
  - Primary Wordmark: `60px` / `22mm` minimum width.
  - Favicon: `16px` / `6mm` minimum height.

---

## 03. LOGO DISCIPLINE & PROHIBITIONS

- **Never** stretch, distort, or skew the V mark or wordmark.
- **Never** convert the V mark into rainbow chrome or flat primary colors.
- **Never** place the logo on bright neon or busy photographic backgrounds.
- **Always** maintain dark obsidian (`#0B0C0E`) or graphite (`#12161F`) backdrop environments.
