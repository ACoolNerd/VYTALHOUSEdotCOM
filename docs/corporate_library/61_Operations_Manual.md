## 01. DAILY FACILITY OPERATIONS & TIMELINE

- **06:00 AM — Morning Opening & Water Testing**:
  - Verify cold plunge water temperatures (38°F–42°F).
  - Test water chemical balance & UV filtration flow rate.
  - Power on Infrared Saunas to 150°F baseline.
  - Perform HBOT & PEMF system diagnostic self-test.
- **07:00 AM — House Doors Open**:
  - Front desk hospitality check-in & adaptogenic tea service.
  - Member intake & towel/robe issuance.
- **09:00 PM — Evening Shutdown & Medical Sanitization**:
  - Complete UV-C surface sterilization across all plunge tubs and saunas.
  - Secure clinical supply room (IV bags, blood collection tubes).
  - Execute nightly POS reconciliation & security lockup.

---

## 02. CLEANING & MEDICAL HYGIENE STANDARDS

- **Cold Plunges**: Continuous 0.1-micron filtration with ozone & UV-C sterilization. Full water change every 72 hours.
- **Infrared Saunas**: Non-porous antibacterial wood treatment. Wipe-down with medical-grade non-toxic disinfectant after every member session.
- **IV Bays**: Medical-grade sterile field prep for every infusion. Disposable single-use needle kits.
