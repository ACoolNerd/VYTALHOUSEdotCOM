## 01. PURPOSE & VISION

VYTAL House operates at the intersection of **science, precision, discipline, recovery, longevity, and premium hospitality**. 

The brand is architected to feel closer to **premium architecture + future wellness + precision technology + luxury hospitality**. It must never feel like a generic gym, a supplement startup, a cheap medical clinic, a spa template, or a bodybuilding brand.

---

## 02. CORE BRAND ATTRIBUTES

Every touchpoint, visual asset, and document produced for VYTAL House must embody the 10 core brand attributes:
1. **Precise**: Highly controlled, pixel-perfect, technical, and deliberate.
2. **Scientific**: Anchored in physician oversight, 180+ biomarker bloodwork, and evidence-based protocols.
3. **Disciplined**: Focused on routine, daily consistency, and compounding advantages.
4. **Elevated**: Sophisticated, architectural, quiet luxury, and timeless.
5. **Enduring**: Built for long-term health trajectory and compounding lifetime momentum.
6. **Premium**: High-grade materials, brushed metals, obsidian stone, and soft-touch packaging.
7. **Modern**: Forward-thinking, sleek, and minimalist.
8. **Intelligent**: Data-driven insights powering personalized recovery algorithms.
9. **Calm**: Acoustic entrainment, water elements, and soft indirect illumination.
10. **Transformational**: Measurable baseline shifts, not vague subjective promises.

### Prohibited Copy Language
Do not use: *"change your life forever"*, *"reverse aging"*, *"miracle cure"*, *"guaranteed results"*, or unverified medical claims.

## 04. GOVERNANCE & APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS

- **File Naming Standard**: `VYTAL_[DIVISION]_[ASSET]_[SIZE]_v01.ext`
- **Clear Space Rule**: Minimum clear space around the V Monogram equal to the width of the inner serif.
- **Color Discipline**: Acid Lime (`#C6FC06`) is strictly a **SIGNAL**; it must never be used as a primary background or dominant title fill.
