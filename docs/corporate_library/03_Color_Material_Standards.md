## 02. ACID LIME SIGNAL RULE

Acid Lime (`#C6FC06`) is classified as a **HIGH-ENERGY SIGNAL**, not a background or primary brand color.

### Approved Uses
- Micro accents & indicator nodes
- Active navigation states
- CTA border glows
- Data visualization nodes & pulse marks
- Heartbeat line pulse points

### Prohibited Uses
- Large background fills
- Full headline text
- Main card backdrops
- Full logo recoloring

## 03. ARCHITECTURAL MATERIAL PALETTE

1. **Matte Obsidian Stone**: Raw, grounded, non-reflective dark stone used for reception desks and accent walls.
2. **Brushed Titanium**: Precision metal finish used for hardware, door pulls, and plaque framing.
3. **Smoked Glass**: Translucent dark glass providing subtle depth for partitions and consultation bays.
4. **Soft-Touch Matte Black**: Tactile, premium finish used for retail tins, boxes, and product canisters.
5. **Black Architectural Stone**: Iridescent-veined dark marble used sparingly in treatment rooms.
6. **Indirect Linear Lighting**: 2700K–3000K warm recessed illumination in stone joints and baseboards.
