How it works

Nine visible steps. No hidden autonomy.

Fill with potable water.
Insert one sealed V-Pod.
Identify formula, lot, expiration and allergen state.
Recommend from the eligible fixed-pod set.
Confirm by direct physical action.
Release the complete serving through the washable pathway.
Mix using the isolated magnetic drive.
Track the event and estimated hydration.
Rinse, remove the insert and reuse.

↗

RESTORE when activity and daily ingredient totals support it.

☾

Evening

RESET or plain water; caffeine is excluded after the member's cutoff.

Gen 1 boundaries: typical routine 1–2 pods daily; maximum 3 pods in 24 hours; maximum one caffeinated pod recommended daily; adults only at launch; suggested 500–750 mL water per pod; no remote release; low-confidence data defaults to ESSENTIAL or plain water.
